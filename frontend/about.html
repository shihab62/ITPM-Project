About this system

A Student Presentation Registration Management System for classes: registration, team management, presentation files, assignments and chat in one place.

Who uses it

Students

Register for a subject alone or as a team, upload the presentation, submit assignments and lab reports, and chat with the course group.

Teachers & CRs

Create subjects with their own registration form, slots, deadline and guidelines, then review registrations.

Admin

Controls everything: teachers, subjects, registrations, settings, public team page, backups and visitor analytics.

What it can do

  • Dynamic registration forms built per subject, individual or team registration, presentation slots and deadlines
  • Guidelines for students on every subject (rules, time, evaluation)
  • Team leaders can edit their team, members and photos until registration closes
  • Presentation upload (PDF / PowerPoint) with a full-screen presenter
  • Assignments and lab reports with file submission
  • Messenger-style chat: course groups and direct messages with teachers / CRs
  • Public team page, public reviews and feedback
  • Admin backup / restore, visitor analytics, installable on a phone (PWA)

Good to know

All times are shown in Bangladesh Time (UTC+6).
